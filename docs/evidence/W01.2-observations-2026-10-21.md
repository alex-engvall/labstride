## Upgrade and recovery

Migrations v3–v5 append to the existing checksum ledger. Legacy objects keep their identities/audit and are not implicitly executable. Terminal results are immutable, resource references remain after workspace removal, and pending projections preserve human changes. Pause effects and take a consistent backup before upgrade. There are no down migrations. A compatible restored database must enter explicit restore mode before workers; unknown effects remain blocked until authoritative evidence resolves them. A lease, timeout, missing resource or unchanged field alone never proves an uncertain effect safe to repeat.
