Recovery controls enforce service exclusion when entering the fence, preserve original identities/epochs and conflicts, and require independent operation observations before resumption. Ordinary run resume cannot clear the installation fence. Reconcile resumes blocked operation/projection/session reads without repeating action starts or clearing conflicts.
